
    AES Encryption Algorithm Skeleton

Step 0: Break File up into bytes and orient into valid input (128, 192, or 256 bit) (column-major ordered matrix)
                (4x4 for 128 bit, 4x6 for 192 bit, 4x8 for 256 bit; always 4 rows no matter block size)

Step 1: Create the Encryption Key (128, 192, or 256 bit)
        -- KeyGeneration

Step 2: Generate Round Keys from the Encryption Key (11 for 128 bit, 13 for 192 bit, or 15 for 256 bit)
        -- KeyExpansion

Step 3: Initial round: 
        -- AddRoundKey

Step 4: Middle rounds: Implement 9 times for 128 bit keys, 11 for 192, and 13 for 256
        -- SubBytes
        -- ShiftRows
        -- MixColumns
        -- AddRoundKey

Step 5: Final Round
        -- SubBytes
        -- ShiftRows
        -- AddRoundKey

Step 6: Take output and convert back into encrypted file
